# Evidence checklist

- Metadata confirms that a provider returned a record; it does not verify a paper's claims.
- Fetched public page content may be an abstract or landing page. State exactly what was accessed.
- Preserve empty results, conflicting records, failures and inaccessible full text.
- Mark model interpretations as unchecked. A source title alone cannot support a conclusion.
- Cite the platform source identifier and preserve source/run/step provenance in the research archive.
