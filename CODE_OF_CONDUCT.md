# Code of Conduct

OpenLab welcomes researchers and contributors across disciplines, languages, backgrounds, identities, and experience levels. Be respectful, discuss evidence, credit other people's work, and make room for disagreement about research and engineering.

## Expected conduct

- Give specific, actionable feedback about work and ideas.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, accessibility needs, and research confidentiality.
- Acknowledge corrections, uncertainty, and differing methods without personal attacks.
- Attribute sources and contributions honestly.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, and knowingly misrepresenting another person's research are unacceptable. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents OpenLab.

## Reporting and enforcement

The initial moderator is [@IsaacHuo](https://github.com/IsaacHuo). For a public moderation request, mention the moderator on the relevant conversation without repeating abusive or private material. For a confidential conduct report, use the contact information the moderator chooses to publish on their GitHub profile. If no private contact is available, request a private contact route without sharing the incident details publicly. Security vulnerabilities use the separate route in [SECURITY](SECURITY.md).

The moderator will review the available evidence and may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban participation for serious or repeated violations. Reports involving the moderator need an independent moderator before a neutral internal review can be offered; that independent route is not yet staffed. There is no guaranteed response time during the initial single-maintainer stage.
